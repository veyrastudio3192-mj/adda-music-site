How playback works

ADDA may use YouTube as a third-party source for music and video playback. Playback runs through permitted YouTube mechanisms, and the content stays on YouTube’s side of the connection.

What ADDA does not do

  • Download YouTube video or audio
  • Store copies of YouTube music
  • Scrape YouTube content
  • Proxy YouTube media
  • Circumvent playback restrictions
  • Remove advertisements or restrictions imposed by YouTube
  • Claim ownership of third-party music

When something is unavailable

A track or playlist can become private, region restricted or removed at any time. ADDA shows a plain fallback state and offers the rest of the playlist rather than working around the restriction.

Trademarks

YouTube is a trademark of Google LLC. The source button in ADDA refers to where music is played from and does not imply endorsement or partnership.

Before release

The implemented integration must be verified against the YouTube terms, API services terms and player policies applicable on the release date.

How the music is chosen

Songs in each Adda are curated by hand from YouTube Music and stored in the app only as a title, an artist credit and a YouTube video id. ADDA has no music library of its own and no way to play a song outside the YouTube player.

Choosing which video to load is the full extent of ADDA's involvement in playback. The app never hides the player, never replaces or duplicates its controls, never changes its volume, never blocks or skips advertising, and never plays YouTube audio in the background or with the screen off. Background playback in ADDA applies only to Ambience Mode, which uses ADDA's own recordings and never touches YouTube.

If you own something we point at

ADDA does not host, store, download or cache any music. Every song listed inside an Adda is played straight from YouTube's embedded player, on YouTube's servers, with YouTube's own controls and advertising intact. The recordings belong entirely to the labels, composers, performers and rights holders who made them, and the titles and artist credits shown in the app are taken from public album and soundtrack listings.

If you hold rights to anything listed in ADDA and would rather it were not listed, write to veyra.studio3192@gmail.com with the song and the Adda it appears in. It will be removed from the next update, no questions asked.
