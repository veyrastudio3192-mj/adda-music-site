Adda Music is an Indian scene and mood app for Android. Each Adda recreates a real place — a truck cabin at night, a chai tapri in the rain, a train journey — through layered ambience, animated art and optional music.

If you own something the app points at

Adda Music hosts no audio. Music plays through YouTube's embedded player, served by YouTube under YouTube's own licensing. If a song is pointed at from inside the app and you would rather it were not, write to veyra.studio3192@gmail.com with the video link and it will be removed from the app's list — usually within the hour, and without a store update. No questions asked.
