Original ADDA content

Scene artwork, illustrations, ambience design, interface copy and the ADDA name are produced for the app. Each asset is tracked in an internal content record noting whether it is original, licensed, open source or third-party, along with its source, licence and any attribution requirement.

Reporting a problem

If you believe content in ADDA infringes your rights, write to the support address in Help and Support (veyra.studio3192@gmail.com) with the details and it will be reviewed.

If you own something we point at

ADDA does not host, store, download or cache any music. Every song listed inside an Adda is played straight from YouTube's embedded player, on YouTube's servers, with YouTube's own controls and advertising intact. The recordings belong entirely to the labels, composers, performers and rights holders who made them, and the titles and artist credits shown in the app are taken from public album and soundtrack listings.

If you hold rights to anything listed in ADDA and would rather it were not listed, write to veyra.studio3192@gmail.com with the song and the Adda it appears in. It will be removed from the next update, no questions asked.
