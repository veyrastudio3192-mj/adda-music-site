Who operates ADDA

ADDA is built and published by Mrityunjay Patel. Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address veyra.studio3192@gmail.com.

The short version

ADDA has no login, no account and no profile. Nothing you do in the app is tied to an identity, and the app does not send your listening history to a server operated by us.

What ADDA stores on your device

These items are written to local app storage so the app remembers where you left off:

  • Favorite Addas
  • Recently played playlists and tracks
  • Recently visited Addas
  • App preferences such as ambience, sound, motion and data saver

What ADDA does not collect

The app does not ask for or collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address or phone number
  • Contacts, SMS or call logs
  • Location, including approximate location
  • Camera, microphone or photo library access
  • Health or fitness data
  • User-generated content, comments or chat
  • Advertising identifier for advertising purposes

Music playback and YouTube

Music plays through permitted YouTube playback mechanisms. When playback starts, requests go to Google or YouTube servers, which receive network information such as your IP address and device details under their own privacy policies.

ADDA does not download, copy, store or re-host third-party audio or video.

Google Play services and billing

If you choose to make a voluntary contribution, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. ADDA receives only the purchase state for that transaction. Payment card details are never handled by the app.

Analytics and crash reporting

This build ships without an analytics or crash-reporting SDK. If either is introduced, this section, the in-app Data Usage page and the Google Play Data Safety declaration must be updated before the release that contains it.

Third-party SDKs

Every third-party library included in the production build is listed under Settings, Licenses and Attributions. Any SDK that transmits data off the device must be documented here before release.

Data sharing

ADDA does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The only outbound traffic in normal use is the playback request to the third-party music source and, if you contribute, the Google Play Billing transaction.

Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you remove it. Settings, Privacy and Data, Delete Local Data clears favorites, recently played, local history and preferences. Uninstalling the app removes the same data.

Because ADDA has no accounts, there is no server-side profile to delete.

Security

App data is kept in the private storage area assigned to ADDA by Android. Network requests for playback use HTTPS.

Children

ADDA is a general-audience app and is not directed to children. The target audience setting declared in Google Play Console must match this statement at release time.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be published at the public policy address before the release that contains them, with an updated date at the top of this page.
